Flylet Changelog

Changelog

What's new in each Flylet version, newest first.

0.13 · November 19, 2026 Latest

  • New volume mixer: an arrow on the volume row opens a slider for every app playing sound. Click an app's icon to mute it.
  • New music visualizer: choose "Music visualizer" as the media card's timeline and it moves with your music. Pick Subtle, Lively or Party, or make your own.
  • New pin button keeps the pop-up on screen.
  • Redesigned color picker with 8 swatches, 4 slots for your own colors and a hex code.
  • New switches to hide the card border and to add a glassy shine to the cards.
  • Firefox's media timeline no longer goes missing.
  • Fixed a crash when a media app closed at the wrong moment.

0.12.1 · November 13, 2026

  • The custom accent color now also applies to the media card.
  • Pictures you've already added are skipped, so no duplicates.
  • Fixed crashes on shutdown, on a second launch, when Explorer restarts, and in media.
  • Brightness updates again after you turn the module off and on.
  • Lock keys no longer runs in the background when it's turned off.
  • A background you removed no longer comes back after a restart.

0.12 · November 9, 2026

  • New Glass option blurs whatever is behind your pop-ups, like frosted glass (Windows 11).
  • Add several photos and GIFs and rotate them each time a pop-up opens, or every 5 minutes, 30 minutes, hour or day.
  • Background opacity now works with your own photos and GIFs.
  • The media timeline now shows whenever an app reports the song length.
  • Flylet now runs on 32-bit (x86) PCs and uses almost no CPU when idle.
  • After an update, a short note links to what's new.

0.11.2 · October 28, 2026

  • New "Use less memory" setting (Settings > General > Performance), which saves about 40 MB.
  • Animated GIF backgrounds use about 12 to 27 MB less memory.
  • Fixed a memory leak in media cards.

0.11.1 · October 27, 2026

  • Flylet no longer closes when music plays in an app running as administrator.
  • If one media app's info can't be read, Flylet skips that app and keeps working.

0.11 · October 9, 2026

  • Redesigned Settings with three tabs (Look, Flyouts, General) and a live preview.
  • Use your own photo, or an animated GIF up to 50 MB, as the pop-up background, with darken and blur sliders and an Adjust photo window.
  • New media card layouts and sizes.
  • The top bar now hides until you point at the pop-up.
  • The brightness slider follows brightness changes made outside Flylet.

0.10.2 · October 6, 2026

  • Opening Flylet from Start or the Store now opens Settings.
  • Custom background colors now show on the brightness pop-up too.
  • The airplane mode pop-up shows the right on/off state.

0.10.1 · September 28, 2026

  • Flylet follows Windows accent color changes right away.
  • New custom accent color and custom pop-up background color.
  • Media buttons turn on and off as the player's abilities change.
  • About 390 missing translations added across all 31 languages.

Full release notes are on GitHub.
